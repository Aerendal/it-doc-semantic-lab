## 1. Definitions

- **"Agreement"** - This contract and all attachments
- **"Services"** - Products/services described in Exhibit A
- **"Customer Data"** - Data provided by Customer
- **"Confidential Information"** - Non-public business information
- **"SLA"** - Service Level Agreement (Exhibit B)

---

## 2. Services

### 2.1 Scope
Vendor shall provide the Services described in **Exhibit A** in accordance with this Agreement.

### 2.2 Service Levels
Vendor shall meet the service levels defined in **Exhibit B (SLA)**.

### 2.3 Changes
Changes to Services require written agreement by both parties.

---

## 3. Term & Termination

### 3.1 Term
- **Initial Term:** [X] years from Effective Date
- **Renewal:** Auto-renews for [1-year] periods unless terminated

### 3.2 Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate with [90] days written notice.

### 3.3 Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate immediately if:
- Material breach not cured within [30] days of notice
- Insolvency or bankruptcy

### 3.4 Effect of Termination
Upon termination:
- Vendor provides transition assistance for [30] days
- Customer data returned/deleted within [30] days
- Accrued obligations survive

---

## 4. Fees & Payment

### 4.1 Fees
As specified in **Exhibit C (Pricing)**.

### 4.2 Payment Terms
- Invoices: [Monthly/Quarterly/Annual] in advance
- Payment due: [30] days from invoice
- Late payment: [1.5]% per month

### 4.3 Price Changes
Vendor may increase prices with [90] days notice, not exceeding [5]% annually.

### 4.4 Taxes
Customer responsible for applicable taxes (excluding Vendor income tax).

---

## 5. Customer Obligations

Customer shall:
- Provide necessary access and information
- Comply with acceptable use policies
- Maintain security of access credentials
- Ensure authorized use only

---

## 6. Intellectual Property

### 6.1 Vendor IP
Vendor retains all rights to Services, software, and pre-existing IP.

### 6.2 Customer IP
Customer retains all rights to Customer Data.

### 6.3 License Grant
Vendor grants Customer non-exclusive license to use Services during Term.

---

## 7. Confidentiality

### 7.1 Obligations
Each party shall:
- Protect Confidential Information
- Use only for Agreement purposes
- Limit access to need-to-know personnel

### 7.2 Exceptions
Confidentiality does not apply to information that is:
- Publicly available
- Independently developed
- Rightfully received from third parties
- Required by law to disclose

### 7.3 Duration
Obligations survive [3] years after termination.

---

## 8. Data Protection

### 8.1 Data Processing
Vendor processes Customer Data only as instructed and per **Exhibit D (DPA)**.

### 8.2 Security
Vendor maintains security measures per industry standards (SOC2, ISO 27001).

### 8.3 Data Location
Customer Data stored in [specified regions].

### 8.4 Breach Notification
Vendor notifies Customer of data breaches within [72] hours.

---

## 9. Warranties

### 9.1 Vendor Warranties
Vendor warrants that:
- Services conform to documentation
- Services do not infringe third-party IP
- Vendor has authority to enter Agreement

### 9.2 Disclaimer
EXCEPT AS STATED, SERVICES PROVIDED "AS IS". VENDOR DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES.

---

## 10. Indemnification

### 10.1 By Vendor
Vendor indemnifies Customer against third-party claims that Services infringe IP rights.

### 10.2 By Customer
Customer indemnifies Vendor against claims arising from Customer Data or misuse.

---

## 11. Limitation of Liability

### 11.1 Cap
Total liability limited to fees paid in prior [12] months.

### 11.2 Exclusions
Neither party liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages.

### 11.3 Exceptions
Limitations do not apply to:
- Breach of confidentiality
- Indemnification obligations
- Gross negligence or willful misconduct
