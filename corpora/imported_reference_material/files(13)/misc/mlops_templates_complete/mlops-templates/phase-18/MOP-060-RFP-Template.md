## 5. Proposal Requirements

### 5.1 Required Sections
1. **Executive Summary** - Overview of your proposal
2. **Company Profile** - Background, financials, references
3. **Solution Overview** - How you meet our requirements
4. **Technical Architecture** - Integration approach
5. **Implementation Plan** - Timeline, resources, methodology
6. **Support & SLA** - Support model, SLA terms
7. **Pricing** - Detailed pricing breakdown
8. **Security & Compliance** - Certifications, practices
9. **References** - 3 relevant customer references
